### 6-BOQICH: GROWTH (O'sish — 6-12 oy)

**Maqsad:** O'sishni tezlashtirish

**Qadamlar:**
1. **Growth Channels** — Qaysi kanallar ishlayapti?
2. **Viral Coefficient** — Har bir mijoz nechta olib keladi?
3. **Retention** — Qaytib kelish %
4. **Revenue** — Oylik daromad
5. **Team** — Kim kerak?

**Growth Hacking texnikalari:**
| # | Texnika | Tavsif | Misol |
|---|---------|--------|-------|
| 1 | **Referral Program** | Tavsiya dasturi | Dropbox ($5/$5) |
| 2 | **Viral Loop** | Avtomatik tarqalish | Hotmail |
| 3 | **Content Marketing** | Foydali kontent | HubSpot |
| 4 | **SEO** | Qidiruv tizimi | Moz |
| 5 | **Social Proof** | Ijtimoiy dalil | Yelp |
| 6 | **Scarcity** | Kamchilik | Gmail (dastlabki) |
| 7 | **Gamification** | O'yinlashtirish | Duolingo |
| 8 | **Network Effects** | Tarmoq effekti | Facebook |
| 9 | **Platform** | Platforma | Shopify |
| 10 | **Community** | Jamoa | Reddit |

**Viral Loop tuzilishi:**
```
1. Mijoz foydalanadi → 2. Mijoz tavsiya qiladi → 
3. Yangi mijoz keladi → 4. Yangi mijoz foydalanadi → 
5. Takrorlanadi...
```

**Viral Coefficient (K):**
- K = (Tavsiyalar soni) × (Conversion rate)
- K > 1 → Viral o'sish
- K = 1 → Barqaror
- K < 1 → Kamayish

**Dropbox misoli:**
- Har bir tavsiya uchun $5 berdilar
- Tavsiya qilgan ham, olgan ham $5 oldi
- Natija: 3,900% o'sish (18 oyda)

**KPI:**
| KPI | Maqsadli qiymat | Manbai |
|-----|-----------------|--------|
| MRR | 20%+ o'sish / oy | Hisob-kitob |
| Viral Coefficient | 1.0+ | Analytics |
| Retention (90 kun) | 30%+ | Mixpanel |
| NPS | 50+ | Survey |
| CAC | Kamayish | Hisob-kitob |
| LTV | O'sish | Hisob-kitob |
